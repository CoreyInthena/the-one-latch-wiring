1 · Buck firstSet the buck to 5.0V alone, with nothing else connected — before the Nano is ever wired in.
2 · Unlock = retractIf unlock EXTENDS the rod, swap the two actuator leads at the relay COMs. Never edit the firmware.
3 · 12V stays on WAGOBattery/actuator power never touches the logic board. Air-gapped, ferrule every stranded end.
4 · Keypad = Dupont onlyNever put heat on the membrane tail — it plugs straight into the screw-board terminals.
5 · Solar orderController → battery FIRST, then the panel (reverse to service). LOAD terminals stay EMPTY by design — the latch keeps its own fused line to the battery.
6 · RTC prepLIR2032 only — the module charges its cell (a CR2032 must never go in). Snip the module's power LED (~13Ah/yr wasted).
